## Overview
Electronics form the invisible backbone of the modern world, controlling everything from the smartphones in our pockets to the life-saving equipment in hospitals. At its core, the field of electronics involves directing electrical currents to process information, communicate, and interact with the physical environment. However, instead of just making traditional devices smaller or faster, our lab is focused on fundamentally reimagining how electronics integrate with our daily lives and even the human body itself. We are pushing the boundaries of what these systems can achieve by making them more intelligent, adaptable, and seamlessly connected to us.

A major part of our research explores how to bridge the gap between rigid technology and soft human tissue using highly sensitive [[Sensors]] and flexible materials. For example, we are developing innovative [[Skin-on Device|Skin-on device]] technologies that conform directly to the contours of the body. This allows for continuous, non-invasive health and movement monitoring without the discomfort of bulky traditional wearables. To make these wearable systems smarter and more autonomous, we are also designing highly efficient [[Neuromorphic Device|Neuromorphic device]] architectures. These brain-inspired circuits can process complex sensory data directly at the source, learning and adapting much like biological neurons, which vastly reduces power consumption and speeds up real-time decision-making.
