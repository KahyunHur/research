# nanowires

## Definition
Nanowires are extremely thin, needle-like structures with diameters measured in nanometers, which is about a thousand times thinner than a human hair. Because they are very long relative to their width, they act as tiny conduits that can efficiently transport electricity or light at a microscopic scale. These unique properties allow them to be used as building blocks for smaller, faster electronics and highly sensitive medical sensors.
