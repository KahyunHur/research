# Polymerization

## Definition
Polymerization is a chemical process where many small molecules, known as monomers, join together to form a large, chain-like molecule called a polymer. This process is essential for creating a wide variety of everyday materials, from natural substances like DNA and proteins to synthetic plastics and rubbers. By linking these smaller building blocks in different ways, scientists can design materials with specific properties such as flexibility, strength, or heat resistance.
