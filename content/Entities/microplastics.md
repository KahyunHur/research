# microplastics

## Definition
Microplastics are tiny pieces of plastic debris, typically less than five millimeters long, that result from the breakdown of larger consumer products or are intentionally manufactured for use in items like exfoliants. Because they do not easily biodegrade, these persistent particles accumulate in oceans and soil, posing significant risks to wildlife and potentially entering the human food chain.
