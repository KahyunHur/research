# Molecular Dynamics

Molecular Dynamics (MD) is an important concept or method in our research. 

Related to: [[Computer-aided materials design]]

## Definition
Molecular Dynamics is a computer simulation method for analyzing the physical movements of atoms and molecules. The atoms and molecules are allowed to interact for a fixed period of time, giving a view of the dynamic "evolution" of the system. We use MD to study polymer melts, self-assembly behavior, and battery electrolytes.
