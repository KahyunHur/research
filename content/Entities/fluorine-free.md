# fluorine-free

## Definition
"Fluorine-free" refers to materials, chemicals, or products that are manufactured without the use of fluorine, a highly reactive element often found in non-stick coatings, water-repellent fabrics, and battery components. Moving toward fluorine-free alternatives is a major environmental goal because many fluorine-based compounds, such as PFAS or "forever chemicals," are toxic and do not easily break down in nature. By eliminating fluorine, scientists aim to create safer, more sustainable products that do not pollute the environment or pose long-term risks to human health.
