# nucleation

## Definition
Nucleation is the initial step in the formation of a new physical structure, such as a solid crystal forming in a liquid or a gas bubble appearing in boiling water. It occurs when a small cluster of atoms or molecules successfully gathers together to create a stable "seed" or starting point. Once this tiny nucleus is established, it acts as a foundation that allows more material to easily attach and the structure to rapidly grow.
