# Neuromorphic device

Neuromorphic device is an important application in our research. 

Related to: [[Electronics]]

## Definition
Neuromorphic devices are electronic components designed to mimic the neural structure and functioning of the human brain. We research three-terminal electrolyte-gated synapses and bio-plausible learning algorithms to create highly energy-efficient hardware for Artificial Intelligence applications.
