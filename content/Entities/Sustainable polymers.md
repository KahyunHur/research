# Sustainable polymers

## Definition
Sustainable polymers are eco-friendly plastics and materials designed to minimize harm to the environment throughout their entire lifecycle. Unlike traditional plastics made from fossil fuels, they are typically created from renewable resources like plants and are built to be easily recycled or biodegraded after use. By breaking down more naturally or being reused, they help reduce global plastic waste and lower our reliance on non-renewable energy.
