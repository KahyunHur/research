# Physical AI

## What is it?
**Physical AI** is the next major frontier of intelligence, where artificial intelligence leaves the screen and enters the physical world. While standard AI (like a chatbot) lives in a digital environment, Physical AI is embedded into tangible systems—robots, drones, smart laboratories, and even the very materials themselves. It is the integration of "brains" (AI algorithms) with "bodies" (physical hardware and sensors).

## Why is it Important?
In our laboratory, Physical AI is the bridge between digital simulations and real-world experiments. It enables the creation of [[Self-Driving Labs (SDL)]] where an AI system can autonomously run a chemical reaction, analyze the results using [[Biosensors]], and then decide what the next experiment should be—all in a closed-loop without human help. 

This approach is critical for several reasons:
- **Autonomy:** Essential for Space Fab, where communication delays make human remote control impossible.
- **Complexity:** Allows us to master complex structures like the Gyroid or tune the performance of an [[Electrolyte-gated Synapse]] by analyzing thousands of variables simultaneously.
- **Efficiency:** By using In-sensor Computing and neuromorphic hardware, Physical AI systems can operate with the extreme energy efficiency of the human brain, which is vital for the transition to [[Sustainable Batteries]] and edge-computing devices.
