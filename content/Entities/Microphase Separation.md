# Microphase Separation

## Definition
Microphase separation is a process where different, incompatible parts of a large molecule try to pull apart, but cannot completely separate because they are chemically tied together. Instead of forming large separate layers like oil and water, they organize into tiny, repeating patterns at the microscopic scale. This phenomenon is often used to create advanced materials, like plastics that combine the strength of one component with the flexibility of another.
