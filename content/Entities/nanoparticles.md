# nanoparticles

## Definition
Nanoparticles are extremely small particles that typically range in size from 1 to 100 nanometers, making them thousands of times thinner than a human hair. Because of their tiny scale, they often exhibit unique physical and chemical properties that are not found in larger versions of the same material, allowing for breakthroughs in medicine, electronics, and energy.
