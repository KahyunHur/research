# High-throughput sequencing

## Definition
High-throughput sequencing is a modern technology that allows scientists to read the genetic code of DNA and RNA much faster and cheaper than traditional methods. By processing millions of small fragments of genetic material at the same time, it enables the rapid analysis of entire genomes or complex biological samples. This approach has revolutionized medicine by making it possible to quickly identify genetic diseases and study biological diversity on a massive scale.
