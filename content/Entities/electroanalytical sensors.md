# electroanalytical sensors

## Definition
Electroanalytical sensors are devices that detect the presence or concentration of specific chemicals by measuring changes in electrical properties, such as voltage or current. They work by converting a chemical reaction on the sensor's surface into an electrical signal that can be easily read and analyzed. These sensors are widely used for portable and rapid testing, such as monitoring blood glucose levels or detecting pollutants in water.
