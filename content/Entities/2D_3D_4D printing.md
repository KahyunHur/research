# 2D/3D/4D printing

2D/3D/4D printing is an important concept or method in our research. 

Related to: [[Additive Manufacturing]]

## Definition
Additive manufacturing encompasses a range of technologies for creating structures layer by layer. 3D printing creates static physical objects, while 4D printing involves smart materials (like active polymers or metamaterials) that can change shape or properties over time in response to external stimuli.
