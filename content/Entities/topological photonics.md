# topological photonics

## Definition
Topological photonics is a field of optics that uses mathematical concepts from topology to guide light in ways that are immune to scattering and defects. By creating "protected" paths, it allows light to flow around obstacles and sharp corners without reflecting back, ensuring remarkably stable transmission. This approach is key to developing robust and efficient optical components, such as lasers and circuits, that are less sensitive to manufacturing errors.
