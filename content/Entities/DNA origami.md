# DNA origami

## Definition
DNA origami is a nanoscale engineering technique where long strands of DNA are folded into specific, complex two- and three-dimensional shapes, similar to folding paper. By programming the natural matching rules of DNA building blocks, scientists can cause these molecules to self-assemble into precise structures. This method is used to create tiny tools, targeted drug delivery vehicles, and custom materials at the microscopic level.
