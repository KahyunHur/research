# Lithium batteries

Lithium batteries are an important application in our research. 

Related to: [[Energy applications]]

## Definition
Lithium batteries (including lithium-ion and solid-state batteries) are rechargeable energy storage devices. Our lab focuses on enhancing their safety, energy density, and sustainability by developing high-voltage cellulose-based solid polymer electrolytes and PFAS-free (fluorine-free) binder materials.
