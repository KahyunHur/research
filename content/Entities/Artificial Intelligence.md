# Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is an important concept or method in our research. 

Related to: [[Computer-aided materials design]]

## Definition
In the context of materials science, AI involves using machine learning and deep learning models (such as Generative AI, Diffusion Models, and Graph Neural Networks) to accelerate materials discovery, optimize inverse design processes, and power autonomous robotic synthesis systems.
