# Polymorphic Clustering

## Definition
Polymorphic clustering is a process where identical elements, such as molecules in a substance or data points in a computer model, group together into multiple distinct structural forms rather than a single uniform arrangement. In physical chemistry and materials science, it describes how particles bond into various shifting patterns instead of locking into one rigid structure. Understanding these different groupings helps scientists predict the diverse physical properties that a single complex material might exhibit.
