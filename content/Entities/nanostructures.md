# nanostructures

## Definition
Nanostructures are incredibly small materials or objects that have at least one dimension measuring between 1 and 100 nanometers, which is thousands of times thinner than a human hair. Because of their tiny size, these structures often exhibit unique physical or chemical properties that are different from larger amounts of the exact same material. These special characteristics allow scientists to use them to create advanced technologies in fields like medicine, electronics, and energy.
