# Catalysis

Catalysis is an important application in our research. 

Related to: [[Energy applications]]

## Definition
Catalysis involves accelerating a chemical reaction using a catalyst. We explore single-atom catalysts supported by polymer nanowires, asymmetric porous structures, and photoelectrochemical methods to drive critical reactions like nitrogen reduction and methane dry reforming at low temperatures.
