# General materials science

## Definition
General materials science is the study of how the internal structure and composition of substances, such as metals, plastics, and ceramics, determine their physical properties and performance. It explores the relationship between how a material is processed and how it behaves under various conditions to improve existing products and develop new technologies. This interdisciplinary field enables the creation of everything from lightweight aircraft components to durable medical implants.
