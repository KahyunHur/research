# Radiative cooling

Radiative cooling is an important application in our research. 

Related to: [[Energy]]

## Definition
Radiative cooling is a passive cooling mechanism where a body loses heat by emitting thermal infrared radiation to the cold universe. Our lab designs optical metamaterials and infrared-transparent polymers that allow for scalable, daytime radiative cooling without requiring energy input.
