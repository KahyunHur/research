# Block Copolymer Self-Assembly

Block Copolymer Self-Assembly is an important concept or material in our research. 

Related to: [[PublicNotes/content/Entities/Self-Assembly]]

## Definition
The spontaneous organization of block copolymers into well-defined nanostructures driven by the thermodynamic incompatibility of the distinct polymer blocks. This bottom-up approach is widely used to engineer multiscale porous materials, photonic crystals, and templates for nanofabrication.
