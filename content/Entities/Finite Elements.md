# Finite Elements

Finite Elements (Finite Element Method, FEM) is an important concept or method in our research. 

Related to: [[Computer-aided materials design]]

## Definition
The Finite Element Method is a numerical technique used for finding approximate solutions to boundary value problems for partial differential equations. It is used in our lab to simulate complex physical phenomena like stress distribution in mechanical metamaterials and heat transfer in radiative cooling devices.
