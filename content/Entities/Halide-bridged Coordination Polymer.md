# Halide-bridged Coordination Polymer

Halide-bridged Coordination Polymer is an important concept or material in our research. 

Related to: [[Coordination polymers]]

## Definition
These are a class of coordination polymers where metal centers are connected by halide (e.g., Cl, Br, I) ligands. They exhibit unique optical, electrical, and thermal properties, making them promising candidates for advanced electronics, sensors, and energy applications.
