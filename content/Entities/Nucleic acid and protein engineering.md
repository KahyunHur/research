# Nucleic acid and protein engineering

Nucleic acid and protein engineering is an important application in our research. 

Related to: [[Biomaterials]]

## Definition
This field involves the design and construction of artificial DNA/RNA nanostructures (e.g., DNA origami) and customized proteins. We utilize these bio-inspired approaches to create highly specific tumor-targeted drug delivery systems, synthetic chaperone enzymes, and programmable nanoscale self-assemblies.
