# Liquid Crystal

Liquid Crystal is an important concept or material in our research. 

Related to: [[Hybrid materials]]

## Definition
Liquid crystals are materials that exhibit a phase of matter with properties between those of a conventional liquid and a solid crystal. They possess orientational and sometimes positional order, allowing them to respond dynamically to external stimuli like electric fields, temperature, or light.
