# Electrolyte-gated Synapse

## Definition
An electrolyte-gated synapse is an artificial electronic component designed to mimic the learning and memory functions of biological brain cells. It uses a specialized material filled with charged particles, called an electrolyte, to control the flow of electricity and adjust its connection strength based on past electrical signals. This mechanism allows advanced computer chips to process information and "remember" patterns in a way that is highly similar to the human brain.
