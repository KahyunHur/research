# Skin-on device

Skin-on device is an important application in our research. 

Related to: [[Electronics]]

## Definition
Skin-on devices (or electronic skin/E-skin) refer to ultra-thin, flexible, and stretchable electronics that can conform directly to the human skin or robotic surfaces. Our focus is on integrating sensors and computing capabilities locally (in-sensor computing) for real-time health monitoring and interactive robotics.
