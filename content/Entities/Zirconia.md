# Zirconia

## Definition
Zirconia is a strong, heat-resistant ceramic material derived from the metal zirconium. Because of its extreme durability and ability to withstand wear, it is widely used in everyday applications like dental implants, artificial joints, and industrial tools. In its crystallized form, known as cubic zirconia, it is also a popular synthetic alternative to diamonds.
