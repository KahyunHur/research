# DFT

DFT (Density Functional Theory) is an important concept or method in our research. 

Related to: [[Computer-aided materials design]]

## Definition
Density Functional Theory is a quantum mechanical modeling method used to investigate the electronic structure of many-body systems, particularly atoms, molecules, and the condensed phases. In our lab, DFT is used to compute the properties of new materials from first principles.
