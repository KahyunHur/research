# Self-Driving Labs (SDL)

## Definition
Self-Driving Labs (SDLs) are advanced research facilities that combine artificial intelligence with robotic automation to independently design and conduct scientific experiments. By analyzing the results of each test in real-time, the AI autonomously decides which experiment to run next, rapidly accelerating the discovery of new materials, chemicals, or drugs. This continuous, self-correcting cycle of planning, testing, and learning allows scientists to achieve breakthroughs much faster than traditional manual research methods.
