**Year:** 2011

## Abstract / Summary
Metamaterials are engineered artificial materials that offer new functionalities such as super-resolution imaging and cloaking. Calculations of the photonic properties of three-dimensionally isotropic metamaterials with cubic double gyroid and alternating gyroid morphologies from block copolymer self-assembly are presented.


## Layman's Summary
Scientists are designing special artificial materials called [[metamaterials]] that can do amazing things, like making objects invisible or creating super-powerful microscopes. In this study, researchers calculated how light interacts with these materials when they are shaped into complex, repeating 3D patterns known as [[gyroids]]. They create these tiny, sponge-like structures using special plastics through a process called [[PublicNotes/content/Entities/Self-Assembly]], where the molecules automatically organize themselves into the perfect shape.
